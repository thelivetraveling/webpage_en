For me, traveling isn’t just about changing countries. It’s about understanding how each place works, exploring it at your own pace, and making the most of every single day.

I’m naturally curious. I enjoy planning optimized routes, researching before I move, and balancing vibrant cities with wild nature. I can go from designing a five-week itinerary across Argentina to analyzing the best season to experience Japan in full bloom. I enjoy the journey itself just as much as the process of preparing it.

I have a technical and structured mindset: I like having things under control, automating what can be automated, and building systems that make traveling easier, more efficient, and more intentional. For me, travel isn’t about wandering aimlessly — it’s about designing meaningful experiences.

On this website, I share real itineraries, practical insights, detailed budgets, and personal reflections. No hype, no unrealistic postcard perfection — just honest planning, smart route optimization, and lived experiences.

If you enjoy well-thought-out travel, a balance between city life and nature, and making the most of every day without losing authenticity… you’re in the right place.
